Gulmarg, Kashmir

Known as the "Meadow of Flowers," this pristine valley offers breathtaking views of snow-capped peaks and lush green meadows. A paradise for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

  • Best Time: Summer (May-July)
  • Highlights: Gondola ride, skiing
  • Elevation: 2,650m

Manali

A charming hill station nestled in the Himalayas, famous for its scenic beauty, adventure sports, and apple orchards. The perfect blend of tranquility and excitement.

  • Best Time: March-June
  • Activities: Trekking, paragliding
  • Famous For: Apple orchards

Lahaul and Spiti

A high-altitude desert valley known for its stark landscapes, ancient monasteries, and unique culture. The challenging terrain makes it a true adventure destination.

  • Best Time: June-October
  • Highlights: Key Monastery, Chandratal
  • Elevation: 3,000-4,500m

Stone Mountain, Atlanta

A natural wonder featuring the world's largest exposed granite monolith. The park offers hiking trails, scenic views, and a glimpse into Georgia's natural history.

  • Best Time: Spring/Fall
  • Activities: Hiking, laser show
  • Height: 825 feet

Agartala, Tripura

The capital of Tripura, known for its rich cultural heritage, beautiful palaces, and lush greenery. A perfect blend of history and natural beauty.

  • Best Time: October-March
  • Highlights: Ujjayanta Palace
  • Culture: Rich tribal heritage

Khoksar, Himachal Pradesh

A picturesque village in the Lahaul Valley, known for its stunning mountain views and traditional Tibetan culture. A hidden gem in the Himalayas.

  • Best Time: Summer months
  • Highlights: Mountain views
  • Culture: Tibetan influence

Kufri, Along the Beas

A scenic hill station known for its apple orchards and panoramic views of the Beas River valley. Perfect for nature walks and photography.

  • Best Time: March-June
  • Highlights: Apple orchards
  • Activity: Nature walks
